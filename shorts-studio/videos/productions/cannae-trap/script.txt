Rome marches eighty-six thousand men onto one field. Hannibal has barely fifty thousand. He is just past thirty, and he has already wiped out two Roman armies. So he does something strange. He pushes his center forward, straight at the Romans, and keeps his best veterans back on the sides. The legions slam into the center. It bends. It falls back. Rome thinks it is winning, and pushes deeper. Then Hannibal's cavalry crushes the Roman horsemen and rides all the way around behind them. The veterans turn inward. The trap snaps shut. Ancient writers count up to seventy thousand Roman dead. In a single day.
