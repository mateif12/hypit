# Brief — Cannae trap (working title)

- **Goal:** an original ~30–45 s English YouTube Short that recreates the *visual and editing
  language* of the War In Motion Gaugamela Short
  (analysis: `../../references/gaugamela-war-in-motion/ANALYSIS.md`), with original content and visuals.
- **Voice:** the user generates the narration themselves in ElevenLabs from `script.txt` and uploads the
  MP3. No AI voice generation, no TTS (Piper or otherwise) and no ElevenLabs API in this project.
- **Cost:** zero. No paid APIs, credits or subscriptions without asking first. Local rendering only.
- **Format:** 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264/AAC.
- **Gates:** (1) the user approves the script → (2) the user uploads the MP3 → (3) production and render.
- **Status:** script delivered and waiting for approval (2026-10-31).
